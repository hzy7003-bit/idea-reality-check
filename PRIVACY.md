# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-10-05**

Idea Reality Check is a static, skills-only Plugin package. It has no server, account connection, database, analytics, telemetry, or publisher-operated data collection.

## Information and purpose

The Plugin publisher does not collect or receive personal information. When you use the Skill, ChatGPT processes the messages and context you choose to provide, which may include a product idea or details about its intended users and use. This information is used by ChatGPT to perform the requested analysis. You do not need to provide your name, contact details, credentials, or other identifying information.

If the host uses web search or browsing to research current products, patents, markets, or regulations, relevant queries may be processed by the host platform and the search providers it uses. This package does not operate or choose those services, and the publisher does not receive the queries or results.

## Recipients

The Plugin itself sends information to no recipient because it contains no network code or service. Messages are processed by the ChatGPT service and, when the host invokes them, its available research tools. Those providers' own privacy terms and controls apply to their processing.

## Retention

The publisher stores no user messages, idea descriptions, search queries, or usage logs; publisher-side retention is zero. Conversation retention and deletion are controlled by the ChatGPT service and the settings applicable to your account. This Skill package cannot inspect or change those settings.

## Your choices

You can choose what information to include, avoid putting confidential or identifying details in a prompt, use the host's available data controls, and delete the conversation through the host. Do not enter passwords, API keys, payment-card data, government identifiers, or private health information into an idea prompt.

## Changes and contact

If this Plugin later adds a service that collects or stores information, this policy and the submission metadata will be updated before that change is released. For questions about the package, use the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/hzy7003-bit/idea-reality-check/issues).
