## Reality Model

Describe the actual usage sequence, constraints, degrees of freedom/data dependencies, edge cases, failure behavior, maintenance, safety, and environmental or platform limits that materially affect this idea.

## Why Existing Solutions Haven't Won

For each explanation, show evidence. Label plausible but unverified causes as hypotheses. Include whether a simpler substitute solves most situations well enough. If the reason is unknown, say so.

## Adversarial Kill

The strongest evidence-backed reasons this idea may fail. Rank by decision impact, not rhetorical force. Separate findings from unresolved risks.

## What Survived

What remains true after the strongest critique? State what evidence would change this conclusion.
